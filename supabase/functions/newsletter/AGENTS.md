- Newsletters are a separate bulk channel: the `newsletter` edge function owns AI drafting, audience resolution, test sends and Resend batch delivery (never the info@ SMTP mailbox, to protect its reputation), with reply-to info@. Recipients are claimed per campaign before each Resend call so retries never mail twice; lost, unsubscribed, invalid and duplicate addresses are always excluded, and every email carries signed one-click unsubscribe headers.
- Roberta (AI Marketing Manager) is separate from Bruno: `newsletter` consumes DB-queued tokenized period runs and only saves drafts; customer sends always require explicit human approval in the ERP. Scheduled news uses one bounded Firecrawl search with dated evidence, stores selected references and never substitutes invented news when research fails.
- Roberta's editorial rules live in a server-only policy module shared by manual and scheduled drafting; strip monetary catalog data when the direct brief does not authorize prices and reject unexpected monetary output. A single low-frequency cron checks regular dates and an occasion calendar; unique period keys and an atomic expiring job lease prevent duplicate or overlapping drafts.
- Newsletter localization is campaign-level, never an AI call per recipient: derive translation targets from the resolved audience, persist text against exact content and news references, require current versions before approval, and snapshot inferred phone/country language per recipient. Phone parsing uses libphonenumber-js; unsupported/ambiguous regions fall back without inventing a locale. Keep manual briefs separate from scheduled news. AI access/credit blocks are persisted server-side and checked at every generation entry point.
- Newsletter photo previews overlay unsaved selections onto the server-rendered email HTML in the frontend; never save or send merely to refresh a preview, and discard stale preview responses so the latest selection wins.
- Newsletter photo management uses a dedicated frontend picker with explicit main/add/remove actions and full-image inspection; selections remain local to the campaign editor until its existing save or approval flow runs.
- Newsletter contact/signature data is deterministic and server-owned in the newsletter contact module, never generated in campaign bodies; the shared preview/test/delivery renderer localizes its contact CTA and retains a protected dark backing for the original white logo.
- Newsletter greetings are deterministic and localized by the shared renderer, never AI-generated per recipient; snapshot the sanitized CRM contact name on audience approval, preserve complete names without guessing first/last boundaries, and keep preview/test sample names local and separate from customer delivery.
- Weekly editorial rotation uses the browser-safe shared newsletter-themes catalog and stable period keys, never generation/send counts; persist the theme on new drafts, constrain search/evidence/prompts to its scope, and localize subject prefixes, rubric labels and next-rubric teasers deterministically. Include the persisted theme in translation signatures; manual campaigns and extra occasions do not consume a cycle slot or relabel existing drafts.
