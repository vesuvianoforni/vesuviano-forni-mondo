- Ready-to-ship requests (`create-ready-to-ship-proforma`) create a shared commercial offer (offer settings, public link + PDF from the offer renderer, no proforma or deposit), email it with photos and a single book-a-call CTA, and queue one Gennaro WaSender follow-up in `ready_to_ship_followups`; `ready-to-ship-followup` claims each row once (never auto-retried), sends inline during Rome working hours and via an hourly Mon–Sat run otherwise, so no polling loop or duplicate WhatsApp.
- The customer offer email is gated by the `ready_to_ship_offer_email` switch in `bruno_automations`: when it is off the offer, public link and PDF are still created and the team notice says the offer must be sent by Gennaro, and no WaSender follow-up is queued because its text refers to an email that was never sent.
