- Bruno chat (`bruno-chat`) operates the ERP through an allowlisted tool loop in `bruno-chat/tools.ts`: CRM edits/next activity, lead creation, orders, and catalog reads; customer messages and offers are delegated to `bruno-agent` and `commercial-offers` with the caller's token so existing validation, idempotency and role checks still apply; prices come only from the active list or the operator.
- The Bruno command center opens on read-only KPIs; its durable bruno_actions ledger owns ERP/Telegram proposals and atomic execution claims. All internal chat writes are proposals; execute stored args by action_id once, preserving underlying message/voice evidence and operation keys. Never replay unknown/running actions. KPI counts use exact role-scoped records and exclude Zapper imports; CRM reminders are not execution authorization. Keep chat, client communications, calls and settings separate.
- Telegram (@Erp_vesuviano_bot, `telegram-bruno`) is internal-team only: chats are paired to ERP users via one-time codes and replies run through `bruno-chat` with a real session of the linked user, so role checks and RLS stay identical to the ERP chat.
  Group chats can be paired too; every member of a paired group acts with the pairing user's ERP permissions.
- Bruno offers always enable the public link; the PDF link is the public page with ?pdf=1 so the customer PDF comes from the same renderer as the preview. Offers are sent through bruno-agent (WaSender by default, or email) and then marked offerta_inviata.
- Bruno's customer persona lives in `_shared/bruno/persona.ts` (base + WhatsApp/email modules + per-stage rules) and is shared by `bruno-chat` and `bruno-agent` suggest, so every customer text follows one identity; editable facts live in `bruno_knowledge` and media in `product_assets` (optional multiple products through `product_asset_links`; Bruno searches every linked product, title and tags) (stored in the public `oven-gallery` bucket under `product-assets/`).
- The Telegram cockpit (`telegram-bruno/cockpit.ts`) owns one fixed card layout for every alert, proposal and result; alerts carry action buttons, same-customer messages within 5 minutes merge into the open alert, and briefings/reminders/proposal expiry run from one hourly weekday tick queued by the database with a per-row token, so the group stays readable and no polling job is added.
- Telegram reads Dial, Chat and Mail through role-scoped paginated coordination tools; direct calls remain browser evidence without invented transcripts. Explicit IT/UK lines override recipient prefixes, and UK UI/Telegram sends share bruno-agent's reserved CRM operation key and stored callback reconciliation to prevent uncertain retries.
